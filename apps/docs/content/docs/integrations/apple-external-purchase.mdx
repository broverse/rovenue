---
title: Apple external purchases
description: What Rovenue records for EU DMA / US link-entitlement purchases, and what it deliberately does not.
---

Apple sends an `EXTERNAL_PURCHASE_TOKEN` notification when a purchase happens
through an external purchase link — the EU DMA and US link-entitlement flows.
Rovenue records it, and this page is explicit about how little that means.

## What the notification actually contains

```
externalPurchaseToken: {
  externalPurchaseId?: string
  tokenCreationDate?: number
  appAppleId?: number
}
```

Apple documents `data`, `summary` and `externalPurchaseToken` as **mutually
exclusive** — a payload carries exactly one. So an `EXTERNAL_PURCHASE_TOKEN`
notification has **no transaction, no product, no price, and nothing that
identifies one of your users**. `appAppleId` is your app; `externalPurchaseId`
is Apple's opaque id for the purchase.

It tells you *a* purchase happened in your app. It does not tell you whose.

## What Rovenue does

Records the token against the project, keyed by `externalPurchaseId` so a
redelivery is idempotent.

## What Rovenue deliberately does not do

- **No subscriber is attached.** There is nothing in the payload to attach it
  to, and inferring one from timing or from the most recent active user would
  produce attribution that looks authoritative and is a guess.
- **No revenue is recorded.** Apple did not process this purchase and does not
  send its amount. A placeholder figure would flow into MRR, LTV and proceeds
  and corrupt all three.
- **No integration event is emitted.** Rovenue's subscription event stream is
  keyed by subscriber, and there is no subscriber here.

If you want an external purchase to affect a user's entitlements or your
revenue reporting, report it yourself: your own checkout knows which user
bought and what they paid, and that is the only place both facts exist.

## Not implemented

- The **External Purchase Server API** — Rovenue does not report tokens back
  to Apple on your behalf, and does not track Apple's reporting deadlines.
- **Per-region commission accounting** for external purchases.

## How this was verified

Against Apple's documented payload shape and the decoder in
`@apple/app-store-server-library`, **not against Apple**. Rovenue's development
environment has no external-purchase entitlement, so this path has not been
exercised end to end with a live notification. Treat it accordingly, and tell
us what you see if you have the entitlement and can.
